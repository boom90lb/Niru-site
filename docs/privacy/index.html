Privacy

The site uses no analytics or application cookies. Contact is by email.

Last reviewed October 14, 2026

Niru ML is an independent research initiative operated by Brendon Reperttang in California, USA.

Visiting the site

The site does not run analytics, advertising trackers, or third-party embeds. Its fonts and scripts are served with the site. It does not set application cookies or create a visitor profile.

The public site is currently served by GitHub Pages. The hosting provider can process ordinary site-delivery information such as network addresses, request paths, and timestamps under its own service terms. The absence of site analytics does not mean the host receives no request data.

Getting in touch

The conversation page links to an email address. It opens your email app; it does not submit a form or send a request to an application database. An email you choose to send contains your address and whatever information you include. Brendon uses that correspondence to consider and reply to your inquiry. The email service also processes delivery information.

Do not email credentials or information you do not have permission to share. Any later collaboration that requires private data needs an agreed process before that data is exchanged.

Questions about your information

For a privacy question or a request to access, correct, or delete correspondence you have sent, write to brendonrt@niru.ml. Use the address associated with the correspondence so the request can be verified.

The former technical-preview form is not offered by this site. If intake or hosting changes, this notice will be reviewed before the changed process is offered.
