ABOUT US

Niru ML is an AI research and solutions company building AMLO (Automated Machine Learning Operations), a framework that automates the most expertise-dependent stages of the ML development lifecycle. We precisely target optimal outcomes while minimizing computational and operational cost.

Most organizations face the same bottleneck when it comes to AI: building and iterating on ML models requires rare, expensive expertise at every stage: model design, training configuration, evaluation, and deployment. AMLO systematizes these decisions, replacing ad-hoc experimentation with principled, compute-aware automation. Your involvement is simply a function of how much you want to customize the goals and data.

WHAT WE BUILD

AMLO is designed as a modular, extensible framework that addresses each phase of the ML pipeline:

Model Design

Traditional model development relies on manual trial-and-error by specialized engineers. AMLO replaces this with systematic, automated exploration of the design space. A validated component registry and intelligent search work together to find architectures precisely tailored to your data, constraints, and performance targets. The result: models that fit the problem, not just the convention.

Evaluation & Validation

Evaluating model candidates is where most compute budgets are wasted. AMLO uses multi-stage evaluation pipelines that screen candidates progressively, investing deeper resources only in the most promising models. Rigorous validation at each stage ensures that results generalize beyond the training set, so you ship models you can trust, not just models that look good on a benchmark.

Operations & Delivery

The gap between a trained model and a deployed product is where many ML projects stall. AMLO provides end-to-end automation from training configuration through deployment: reproducible pipelines, checkpoint management, and structured delivery artifacts. Teams go from concept to production with minimal operational friction, and every run produces a full reproducibility record.

IS THIS FOR YOU?

We work with organizations that have ML ambitions but are constrained by the gap between available expertise and the complexity of modern model development. Whether you need a custom model tailored to your domain or want to reduce time-to-production for your ML team, AMLO provides the infrastructure to get there efficiently.
